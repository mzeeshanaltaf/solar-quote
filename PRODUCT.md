# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners, globally, holding their latest electricity bill and wondering if solar is worth it. They arrive skeptical: solar marketing is pushy, quote sites harvest their phone number before showing anything useful. Mobile is the dominant context (photographing a paper bill with a phone camera). They are not energy experts; they know two numbers: what they pay and roughly what they use.

A secondary user is the operator (admin) who reviews captured leads in a dashboard. That surface is product-register and is designed separately in later phases.

## Product Purpose

SolarQuote turns an uploaded electricity bill into a personal solar savings estimate: system size, monthly/annual savings in the bill's own currency, payback period. The full result is shown ungated; lead capture is a soft, optional ask afterward. Success = a homeowner trusts the numbers enough to ask for installer quotes.

## Brand Personality

Warm, literate, unhurried. Three words: sunlit, candid, editorial. The interface should feel like a well-set magazine feature about your own house, not a SaaS funnel. Emotional goals: calm confidence ("these numbers come from my own bill"), zero sales pressure.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS look: purple/indigo gradients, glassmorphism, dark hero with glowing orbs, hero-metric cards, identical icon-card grids.
- Solar-industry clichés: stock photos of panel installers giving thumbs up, aggressive "SAVE 90% NOW" urgency banners, gated results behind phone-number walls.
- Cold corporate fintech: navy + neon, dense dashboards on the marketing page.

## Design Principles

1. **The bill is the hero.** Every claim traces back to the user's own document; the design foregrounds their numbers, not our brand.
2. **Show, then ask.** Full results before any contact request. The CTA is an invitation, never a tollgate.
3. **Sunlight, literally.** Warm whites, amber/ochre, deep ink. The palette is the product's subject.
4. **Editorial calm.** Serif display headlines, generous whitespace, long-form rhythm. Reads like print, works like software.
5. **Global by default.** Currency, language of the bill, and location are the user's; nothing assumes US-centric formats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Mobile-first (camera upload is the dominant path). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color is never the only signal (confidence highlights, statuses). Touch targets ≥ 44px in the funnel.
